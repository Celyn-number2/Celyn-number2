## About Me

- Building **AI-assisted products** for music, learning, and personal publishing.
- Creating **MusiCue FM**, **ContextVocab**, and **Firefly Hub**.
- Working mainly with **TypeScript, Node.js, Python, Next.js, and local-first systems**.
- Interested in **LLM workflows, contextual interfaces, learning systems, and developer tools**.
- I care about turning AI capabilities into products that are practical, understandable, and pleasant to use.
- Writing project notes and experiments at [Celyn's Blog](https://www.celyn-blog.xyz/).

## Right Now

- 🎧 Building [MusiCue FM](https://github.com/Celyn-number2/MusiCue), a local-first AI music player that turns mood, scenes, and conversational requests into listening queues.
- 📚 Expanding [ContextVocab](https://github.com/Celyn-number2/English_Context) around continuous story learning, AI-generated context, and SM-2 spaced repetition.
- ✍️ Improving [Firefly Hub](https://github.com/Celyn-number2/Firefly_Hub) and publishing project notes and experiments on [Celyn's Blog](https://www.celyn-blog.xyz/).
